Some calls were close:

- **Statement 6** is a consequence of the specific Sunday episode (redoing Thursday), so it stays in A.
- **Statement 12** points at a problem (leftovers) without telling a story about it. It is an opinion and a topic to probe, so it goes in D.

Torres treats group A, the specific past story, as the most reliable evidence. Describing what actually happened on one occasion reveals real behaviour, context and workarounds, whereas generalisations, predictions and feature requests are filtered through what people think they do or would do, and are often wrong.
