## Rewritten first question

> **"Tell me about the last time you planned your meals for the week. Where were you, what were you trying to get done, and what did you do first?"**

Why it works: it asks for one specific, recent event instead of a habit or an opinion. It says nothing about Larder or any feature, so the story isn't steered. The sub-prompts ("where were you, what did you do first") pull concrete sequence and context.

Good follow-ups once she's telling the story:

- "What happened next?"
- "You said you check prices first. Walk me through the last time you did that. What were you looking at, and what did you decide?"
- "Was there a moment last week when the plan didn't work out? What did you do instead?"

**Coaching summary:** this is interview 1 of the 3-4 Torres recommends before mapping opportunities. Re-interview with story-based prompts, and save feature and pricing reactions for later assumption tests, where behavior (a fake-door click, say) is more reliable than a stated "yes".
